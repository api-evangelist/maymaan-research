# MayMaan Research

> MayMaan Research is a clean-energy engineering company in Hollywood, Florida that
> redesigns the internal combustion engine around its patented AquaStroke platform — a
> piston engine burning roughly 70% water and 30% bio-alcohol (ethanol or methanol)
> instead of gasoline or diesel. It manufactures modular generator sets and powertrains
> from 35 kW (A35) through the AV2 to multi-megawatt installations, sold as drop-in
> replacements for diesel gensets.

## Agent-relevant summary

- Products: AquaStroke combustion platform; A35 (35 kW generator); AV2.
- Fuel: water and bio-alcohol blend, approximately 70/30. No gasoline or diesel.
- Claimed output: near-zero NOx, SOx, CO2 and particulate emissions.
- Scale: modular, 35 kW to 3+ MW; positioned as drop-in for existing diesel setups.
- Markets: power generation, EV charging infrastructure, data centers, telecom,
  marine, construction, agriculture, government and defense, off-grid power.
- Offices: Hollywood, Florida (US) and Paris (Europe).
- Funding: $30M Series A led by WAVE Equity Partners.
- Engagement model: sales-led. There is no self-service signup; the commercial entry
  point is a quote request form, not a pricing page or an API key.
